# Copy quality

Use this for a bounded copy request, not as a substitute for product truth.

- Start from the supplied audience, action, facts, and surface.
- Keep qualifiers such as “when capacity allows” or “available in selected regions.”
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over generic benefit language.
- Remove unsupported superlatives, fake urgency, invented proof, and keyword repetition.
- Keep titles and descriptions distinct from visible headings when the surface needs both.
- Check visible text, metadata, alt text, aria labels, and empty states together.
- Report facts that need owner confirmation instead of filling gaps by invention.
