# Team playbook

Follow the current owner procedures. Role authority comes from `config/manager-state-contract.json` and `runtime/prompts/`.

1. [Install and initialize](getting-started.md).
2. [Import issues](issue-sources.md) or [plan a request](plan-studio.md).
3. [Review contracts and queue membership](tasks-and-queues.md).
4. [Check providers](providers.md) and [resource policy](resource-policy.md).
5. [Preview, confirm, and monitor a bounded run](overnight-runs.md).
6. [Review changes](task-change-review.md), current checks, and independent review.
7. [Recover failures](queue-recovery.md), preserving evidence.
8. [Record lessons](brain.md) and inspect [reports](execution-reports.md).

Use [agent team](team.md) for responsibilities, [interface reference](team-interfaces.md) for menus, and [resource model](resource-model.md) for persisted records. Local branch, PR, merge, and deployment are separate outcomes.
