## Outcome
Use comparable accepted outcomes to improve quality and cost after the first release. Make review burden and unknown telemetry visible. Introduce stronger quotas before expanding concurrent paid execution; do not infer savings from cheap individual calls.

## Ordered work inside this phase
1. Join attempts, repairs, review, verification, human decisions and final acceptance from receipts; report denominator, coverage and actual observed usage. Do not price unknown usage as zero.
2. Add reserve/settle accounting at controllable tool/API admission boundaries, durable windows and crash/unknown settlement semantics. Retry/failover cannot reset allowances. Only claim enforcement for calls actually routed through the boundary; external host calls remain explicitly outside it.
3. Add review-WIP/backpressure and a small actionable decision queue; stop accumulating changes faster than a developer can inspect them. Keep this policy configurable and grounded in observed burden.
4. Build a local held-out review dataset with known defects, healthy controls, borderline cases and reproducibility labels. Compare true/false positives, misses, review delay and accepted-outcome cost. Include all failed/repair attempts.
5. Run bounded opt-in comparisons and ablations before changing model routing, review depth or context. Retain a simpler harness if extra scaffolding does not improve outcomes.

## Control Plane
Extend existing resource policy, usage/execution reports and notifications. Show accepted/attempted counts, failures, manual interventions, wait/review time, usage coverage and quota state. A forecast is labeled as a forecast; it cannot replace observed spending or create authority.
